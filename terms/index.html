What the service is

Beatplan reads the sales history and customer list you import and produces a daily round: who each person should see, why, and what is worth selling them. It emails that round to your team each morning and keeps a record of what they logged.

It is a tool for deciding where to send people. It is not accounting software, it does not replace your invoicing, and the figures in it are only as current as the data you import.

The trial
