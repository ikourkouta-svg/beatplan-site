Beatplan is a sales and operations assistant for distributors and wholesalers. It turns your sales history into a daily call list, so nobody has to remember which customer has gone quiet.

Open the app See pricing

What it actually does

No dashboards nobody opens. A list, in order, with a reason next to each name.

A queue, not a database

Every customer has a rhythm. When one falls behind their own pattern, they appear at the top of the list with the reason and what they usually buy.

Works with no signal

Built for people in a vehicle. Calls and orders log offline and sync when the phone finds a connection again.

Stock that argues back

It will not push what you are short of, sells the oldest batch first, and raises an alarm when a price sits below cost.

Debtors in the same place

Who owes what, how old it is, and who promised to pay when. Logged from the same screen as the call.

A morning email

Who to chase, what is expiring, what needs reordering. Sent before the day starts, so the plan exists before anyone opens a screen.

A monthly view for owners

Revenue against the same month last year, which customers grew, which declined, how the debtor book is ageing, and what stock is dead.

Getting your data in

Export your customers, products and invoice history from whatever you use now, as a spreadsheet or a CSV. Beatplan reads the column headings, shows you what it understood, and lets you correct it before anything is saved. History matters: it is what the call list is built from.
